# Settle fresh memories

Each note below is a fresh memory: captured recently and not yet in a place of its own. Overnight
it settles where it belongs, the way the day's memories settle while we sleep. For each note,
choose one of its home notes (a topic or project note it will sit beside, and which will link to
it), one of its folders, or nowhere yet.

## Choosing

- Prefer a **home note** when the note is part of what that home is about: a finding about the
  payments system goes beside "Payments", which then links to it.
- Choose a **folder** when the note fits an area but no single home: a recipe into `kitchen/`.
- Go by what the note is about and what the user will look for it under, not by shared words.
- Choose `none` when nothing clearly fits. The note stays fresh and is looked at again tomorrow,
  when the notebook may have a better place. A note in the wrong place is harder to find than
  one in no place.

## The reason

One plain sentence for the user: "A tuning plan for the orders table, which belongs to the
payments system."
