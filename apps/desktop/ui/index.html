    Inbox

    What you captured and haven't dealt with. Keep it, archive it, or let Dream gather it overnight.

      Dream

      While you sleep, Dream compresses the notebook: it merges copies, gathers fragments, retires what nothing uses and packs old archives. It proposes; you decide. Everything can be undone.

        Archive

        Notes that left the notebook. They're still searchable (ranked lower) and come back with one click, even from a pack.

          History

          Every change FastMind made to your notes. Undo puts the files back as they were, unless you've edited them since.
