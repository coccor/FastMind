            Model

            FastMind's AI writes up each capture, answers your questions from your notes, and decides overnight what to merge, gather, archive and settle.

            Each capture is saved as you typed it at once, then written up a few seconds later: title, summary, key points, tasks, related notes and where it belongs. A note you've edited is never rewritten, the original is always kept, and History undoes it.

            Skills

            How the AI judges and writes, one skill per job. They decide the quality of your notes. Edit one to change it for this notebook; FastMind always adds today's date, your preferences, and the rule that notes are never instructions.
